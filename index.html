About

Bridgton Road Investments LLC ("Bridgton Road") is a concentrated global long-only investment firm. Our strategy specializes in identifying niche market leaders with defendable competitive advantages. We seek out smaller-scale monopolies — companies that have built impenetrable moats within their specific market segments.


Through deep fundamental research and a focused portfolio of carefully selected companies, we aim to compound capital at exceptional rates over long holding periods.
